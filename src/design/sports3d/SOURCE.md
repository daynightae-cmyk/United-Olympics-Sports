## Production rule

No temporary Figma export URL may be used by production UI. A 3D asset is enabled only after:

1. its exact source layer is identified;
2. its semantic meaning exactly matches the registry key;
3. intended-use licensing is reviewed; and
4. the approved file is localized under `/public/media/sports-3d/` and registered in `sports3d.registry.ts`.

This is intentionally fail-closed. In particular, **Gymnastics is not mapped to Gym** and **Martial Arts is not mapped to Boxing**.
